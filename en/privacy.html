Privacy notice

A short notice on how Westersol handles the details you send through the intake form.

What we collect

The intake form asks for your name, email, phone, budget, timing, preferred area, type of property and financing, as well as your consent.

Why

To handle your request and, only if you later agree to a specific introduction, to share your details with one vetted partner agency. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR). We do not use the data for any other purpose.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We pass it to a partner agency only after a separate, recorded consent for that introduction.

How long we keep it

We keep requests for as long as we need to handle them, and then only for as long as a later introduction or a legal obligation requires.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction or deletion, or withdraw your consent, through the intake form or by writing to contact@westersol.com. Controller: Westersol, reachable at the same address.
