Three towns, one stretch of coast

Westersol introduces buyers to trusted local agents between San Pedro de Alcántara and Sotogrande. These town guides help you choose where to look, with recent asking prices and the same explanation of the NIE, taxes and costs. We then introduce you to one agent we have vetted. You pay us no fee.

Ask to be introduced Buying guide

Each place has a different pace. San Pedro is a seaside town with a promenade. Estepona has an old town, a marina and many newer neighbourhoods. Sotogrande is more spacious and quieter, oriented towards golf, polo and the marina. Read the town that draws you, or start with the buying guide if you want the process first.

Sotogrande

Marina, golf and space, in the municipality of San Roque.

4,110 euros/m² in October 2026

San Pedro de Alcántara

A seaside town, with a promenade and Guadalmina next door.

4,765 euros/m² in October 2026

Estepona

Old town, marina and hills, the largest town on this stretch.

5,070 euros/m² in October 2026
