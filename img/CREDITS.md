## Map

`img/map/coast-map.webp` is an original illustrated map of the western Costa del Sol. Town labels are overlaid in [Patrick Hand](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Patrick+Hand) (Patrick Wane, [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://scripts.sil.org/OFL)).

Benahavís is not named on the site, so it has no photo. Guadalmina and Alcaidesa are mentioned only in passing and were not given their own photos.
